Spatial Innovations Yvette Gong 21 Aug 2026 The Challenge: Unorganized facility data and CAD-dependent workflows make data inaccessible Critical facility data is often scattered across CAD software, files, spreadsheets and paper records. As a result, the people responsible for day-to-day facility operations struggle to locate and use the information they need. Using the CAD-centric faciliCAD Desktop application, the employees of one Spatial Innovations client, for example, could only access important data during the two half-days a week that a CAD-trained intern was in the office.
