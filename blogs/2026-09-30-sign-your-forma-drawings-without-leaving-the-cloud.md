Sign Your Forma Drawings Without Leaving the Cloud Joao Martins 1 Oct 2026 João Martins Share Introduction A sheet is ready for approval. It lives in Forma, next to the rest of the project. Getting it signed usually goes like this: export the sheet as a PDF, download it, upload it to your e-sign tool, type in the signer's email, wait, download the signed copy, then upload it back to the right Forma folder.
