APS introduces proactive quota notifications that alert administrators when their team approaches or reaches API quota limits, with two notification types: "nearing quota" warnings and "quota reached" alerts. Notifications are delivered through both the Autodesk Accounts Portal and the APS Developer Portal so teams can act before applications are affected.
